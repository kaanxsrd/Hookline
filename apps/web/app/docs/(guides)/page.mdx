# Introduction

Hookline gives you a URL that captures any HTTP request. You can watch requests arrive live, inspect them, replay them, forward them to your servers and pipe them to localhost through a CLI tunnel.

## Capture your first webhook

1. Open [Get a webhook URL](/new). You get a temporary endpoint right away, no account needed. It lasts 24 hours and keeps up to 100 requests.
2. Send it something:

   ```sh
   curl -X POST https://<your instance>/h/<slug>/orders \
     -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
     -d '{"id": "ord_123", "status": "paid"}'
   ```

3. The request shows up in the inbox within about a tenth of a second, with its method, path, headers, query and body.

To keep the endpoint, [sign up](/sign-up). During onboarding you can claim it into your new organization along with everything it captured.

## Send webhooks to your laptop

Install the [CLI](/docs/cli), sign in once, and forward an endpoint to your local server:

```sh
hookline login
hookline listen <slug> --forward http://localhost:3000/api/webhooks
```

Every request the endpoint captures is now also sent to `localhost:3000`, and your server's status code is printed next to it. The endpoint keeps capturing while you are offline, so you can [replay](/docs/replay) anything you missed.
