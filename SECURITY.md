# Security policy

Hookline stores webhook payloads, which often carry tokens, email addresses and payment data. We take reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue**. Report privately through GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/kaanxsrd/Hookline/security/advisories/new) for this repository.

Include:

- what is affected (component, route or command, and version or commit),
- steps to reproduce or a proof of concept,
- the impact you expect.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 3 business days.
- An assessment and a planned fix date within 10 business days.
- Credit in the advisory and changelog when the fix ships, unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

Please give us a reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly. Good-faith research that avoids privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption will not lead to legal action.

## Scope

In scope: this repository's code (web app, edge server, worker, CLI) and its default configuration.
Out of scope: denial of service by volume, social engineering, and findings that need a compromised machine or account.
